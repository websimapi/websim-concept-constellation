Concept Constellation

Explore the web of related concepts. Enter a word and see what's connected!

Enter a word above and click 'Explore' to see the concept constellation.

How to Use

Enter a word into the input box above and click the 'Explore' button (or press Enter). The visualization area will display a graph:

  • The large green circle in the center is the word you entered.
  • The smaller blue circles around it represent concepts related to the center word.
  • Lines connect the center word to its related concepts.
  • Click on any of the smaller blue circles (related concepts) to make that word the new center and explore *its* related concepts!

What's Next? (Ideas for Future Development)

This is a basic demonstration, but here are some ideas for expanding the Concept Constellation:

  • **Richer Data:** Using a larger, more sophisticated knowledge base or AI model to find more accurate and diverse relationships between words.
  • **Multi-Layer Exploration:** Allowing users to see concepts related to the related concepts, creating a deeper, multi-layered graph.
  • **Interactive Visualization:** Adding animations for smoother transitions between graphs, enabling dragging of nodes, and potentially different layout options (like force-directed graphs).
  • **Concept Information:** Displaying definitions or brief descriptions when hovering over or clicking a concept node.
  • **Search & Filter:** Allowing users to search for specific concepts within a displayed constellation.
  • **Saving & Sharing:** Features to save interesting constellations or share them with others.

Feel free to suggest other ideas!
