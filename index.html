Concept Constellation

Explore the web of related concepts. Enter a word and see what's connected!

How to Use

Enter a word into the input box above and click the 'Explore' button (or press Enter). The visualization area will display a graph:

  • The large green circle in the center is the word you entered initially.
  • Click on any of the smaller blue circles (related concepts) to explore *its* related concepts. These will be added to the existing graph, allowing multi-layer exploration.
  • Lines connect related concepts.
  • **Drag nodes:** You can click and drag the nodes to rearrange the graph layout.

What's Next? (Ideas for Future Development)

This is a basic demonstration, but here are some ideas for expanding the Concept Constellation:

  • **Richer Data:** Fetching more detailed information about concepts using APIs or AI. **-> Implemented (AI provides related terms)**
  • **Multi-Layer Exploration:** Allowing users to see concepts related to the related concepts, creating a deeper, multi-layered graph. **-> Implemented**
  • **Improved Visualization:** Adding animations for smoother transitions, improving node positioning to reduce overlap, enabling dragging of nodes, and potentially different layout options (like force-directed graphs) for better clarity as the graph grows. **-> Implemented basic force layout, dragging, transitions, increased link distance.**
  • **Concept Information:** Displaying definitions or brief descriptions when hovering over or clicking a concept node.
  • **Search & Filter:** Allowing users to search for specific concepts within a displayed constellation.
  • **Saving & Sharing:** Features to save interesting constellations or share them with others.
  • **Node Styling:** Different colors or styles for nodes based on their "level" from the original center or their type.

Feel free to suggest other ideas!
